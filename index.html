rclone personal Drive client

A single-user OAuth client for rclone.

This page exists to satisfy Google's requirement that an OAuth application publish a homepage and a privacy policy before it can leave "Testing" status.

The application is a personal, single-user configuration of the open-source file-sync tool rclone. It is used by its owner to copy their own files to and from their own Google Drive. It is not offered to other people, has no other users, and collects nothing.
